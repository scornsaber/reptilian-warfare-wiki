REPTILIAN WARFARE / PLAYER GUIDE

Gila

A ground predator that digs, climbs and fights with venom and fire.

Ember Altar

Found in winding underground cave settlements.

Abilities

  • Left-click to bite and apply venom. Other gilas can still be poisoned.
  • Hold right-click to dig forward; add Sneak to dig down or Jump to dig up.
  • Hold Jump while pushing against a wall to climb. Full Gila does not use an ordinary jump.
  • Tap Sprint for a dash, including in the air. Recharge: 60 seconds.
  • Hold B to channel fire near your starting point. Releasing, moving away, climbing, dashing, running out of energy or reaching the channel limit stops it. A damaging hit alone does not automatically cancel the fire.

Fire becomes wider and has a longer recharge as you grow. It can ignite wood and creatures, but cannot destroy fortified wall materials; use digging to breach eligible walls. Gila Burn lasts 20 seconds and is stronger than normal burning.

Feeding and eye

Feed in source lava. Gilas resist their fiery environment and do not inherit Ocean swimming. The Gila eye grants immunity to fire, lava and Gila Burn, plus stronger burning on successful melee and ranged attacks. It grants tusks, not venom immunity.
