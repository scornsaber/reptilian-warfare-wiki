World & settlements

Five kaiju habitats, fortified hunter villages, and territory worth taking.

SpeciesSettlement
Ocean BeastSunken city ruins made from Void Bulwark, with Tide Altars, spires and radiation patches.
GilaWinding underground tunnels, lava and Ember Altars.
Mountain SerpentCaves high on mountain sides, wide entrances, ledges and Mountain Altars. Access requires climbing or flight.
FrostbackFrozen cave settlements and Frostback Altars. Broods, warrens and courts are slightly less common in newly generated terrain.
Solar WormDesert ruins and Sun Altars.

Kaiju settlements come in brood, warren and court sizes. Larger domains have stronger residents.

Visit or raid

Normal human visitors can approach residents without starting a fight, but residents retaliate if attacked. Use the altar with an empty hand to choose its species; you do not need to defeat its residents to unlock it. Hunters and rival species are enemies. Friendly residents protect their own species.

Hunter settlements

Hamlets, towns and cities have fortified floors and walls, with grass patches and farms. Townsfolk refill independently of hunter replacement: up to one missing civilian every 15 seconds, provided the heart is intact, a safe spot exists and no kaiju is inside.

Village hunters respond to kaiju across the whole walled area and stay inside their walls. Small raids are most common; devastating raids are rare. Each kaiju raid chooses one species from the full roster. Hunters also raid kaiju domains, including mountain settlements.

Capture instead of destroying

  1. Reach kaiju stage 6 or hunter King rank.
  2. Defeat the defenders required by the heart or altar’s normal destruction rules.
  3. Empty both hands, look at the rival heart or altar, and hold right-click for ten seconds.

Releasing or losing the target cancels progress. Digging and other abilities do not fire during capture. Buildings, floors and terrain remain unchanged; the anchor and defenders change faction. Captured settlements replenish combatants only, never townspeople.

At a hunter heart, the destruction rules count its hunters, guards and king; golems and ballistas are not additional capture requirements. At an altar, nearby same-species residents count as defenders.

A land kaiju cannot capture a submerged Ocean settlement. Ocean kaiju and hunters can; capture never dries out or rebuilds the terrain.

No endless reinforcements during battle

Captured settlements stop replacing casualties while enemies, active defender combat or an approaching raid are present. They need 30 quiet seconds before recovery resumes. A last defender’s death does not erase the attack state. This also applies when attacking a previously captured domain as another species.

Use Ocean’s breach attacks, Gila digging, Mountain lasers, Frostback’s front stomp or Solar bolts. Gila fire does not burn through these walls. Hearts and altars still need their defenders defeated.

Find a suitable opponent

Wild full kaiju range from stage 2 to 6. Encounters mix random sizes with occasional progression targets. After roughly ten eligible minutes without a suitable nearby opponent, a future valid spawn can prioritize one. A kaiju player’s timer runs only in full form; hunters have no transformation requirement. Terrain still has to support the spawn, and this does not guarantee an immediate raid for the final hunter trial.

Older worlds

New structure placement affects new chunks only. Existing settlements and spires stay put. New standalone void spires remain a deep-ocean feature; Ocean domains also have their own radiation sources.
