Pick the matching build

Choose your side

To become a kaiju, find its settlement and use the altar with an empty hand. You begin at stage 1 with a half form. Choosing a species makes other species' altars refuse you.

To become a hunter, bring an eye from any species to a Hunter King and accept the eye-for-chestplate trade. Hunters cannot use altars to unlock forms, but a third active eye offers a separate route to becoming a beast. Read Betrayal before trying it.

Normal human visitors can approach domain residents peacefully. Attacking them starts a fight; hunters and rival kaiju are enemies. Terrain hazards still matter.

Useful first checks

Press H for progression and appearance. Press V for half form. From stage 2, hold G for five seconds to change full form. Transform before entering a fortified human village.

Updating an older world

Back up your save first. Existing form unlocks are preserved. Older growth stages round up to the next matching strength milestone in the six-stage system, so earned power is not rounded away. Explore new chunks for new settlements; existing buildings are not regenerated.
