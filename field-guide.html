Field guide

A practical reference for your next adventure: choose a side, grow stronger, and take territory.

These gameplay rules apply to 3.7.1 and the Minecraft 26.3 port. Default keys and settings are shown. The expanded in-game book ships with the port.

Choose your path

To become a kaiju, approach its altar as a peaceful human and use it with an empty hand. You choose one species. You do not need to kill the residents to unlock it. Hunters cannot unlock forms at altars.

To become a hunter, trade any species' eye to a Hunter King for a Scale Chestplate. Keep returning to a King for promotions and replacement rank gear.

Press H for your current objective and appearance. V toggles half form. From stage 2, hold G for five seconds to change full form. B uses your species power; R roars in full form. See all controls.

Five upgrades, six stages

  1. Stage 1 to 2: defeat 10 mod Townsfolk while in half form. Vanilla villagers and cultists do not count.
  2. Stage 2 to 3: defeat a stage-2-or-higher full kaiju while fully transformed.
  3. Stage 3 to 4: break a hamlet heart at stage 3.
  4. Stage 4 to 5: break a town heart at stage 4.
  5. Stage 5 to 6: break a city heart and kill a stage-5-or-6 full kaiju while fully transformed. Complete both at stage 5, in either order.

Early heart victories are not saved for later upgrades. Half-kaiju kills do not satisfy a full-kaiju objective. Stage 6 is the cap and keeps the old stage-11 maximum strength. Existing saves round old stages upward to preserve earned power.

Wild full kaiju range from stage 2 to 6. After about ten eligible minutes without a suitable target nearby, a valid future spawn may favor a progression opponent. The timer runs only while fully transformed for kaiju; hunters have no form restriction. It is not an instant spawn or a guaranteed village raid.

Half and full forms use beast energy. Destroying an eligible heart or rival altar fills it. Return to human form for ordinary food. Player kaiju share regeneration rates at matching stages; NPC kaiju have no passive regeneration.

Tap Sprint to dash in your look direction, even in the air. It recharges in two seconds and improves with rank. Wear a Scale Chestplate to fire the crossbow; worn scale armor powers it. Bring a Scale Spear and Crossbow to a Hunter King to forge a Lance.

From rank 2, press X for a levy. Troops stay five minutes; the recharge is 20 minutes. Each borrowed eye lowers your effective levy rank by one. Your own summoned troops stay loyal. See the troop table.

Power has a cost

Use an eye as a hunter to consume it and activate its power. Your first two must be different species. Spare eyes in your inventory do not affect your standing.

  • Ocean: radiation immunity, 65% Ocean beam resistance, and radiation on attacks.
  • Gila: fire, lava and Gila Burn immunity; attacks cause a stronger 20-second burn. Venom still works against gilas.
  • Mountain: ascent and gliding, with wings and horns.
  • Frostback: freeze and Chilled protection, with chilling attacks.
  • Solar: marking attacks; a later hit can consume the mark for a small bonus, at most once every two seconds.

Attack effects apply to melee and ranged hits. Eyes do not give full-form beams or breath. Armor and hunter rank do not grant radiation immunity.

With no eyes, village defenders protect you. One eye removes protection. Two make the garrison hostile. Sneak + Use a matching eye to remove that power without consuming the held eye; the original eye is not refunded.

Betrayal

With two eyes active, hold Use on any third eye for five seconds. You immediately become its stage-2 full kaiju and lose hunter rank, borrowed powers and your levy. The last eye may be a duplicate. Release early to cancel.

The Outcasted King and four renegade hunters roam together. Each carries two random, different eye powers and can dash. They fight ordinary hunters and kaiju. Their king does not recruit you or replace gear.

Break through or take over

Hunter villages have protected walls and floors. Their hunters respond throughout the walled area and stay inside. Kaiju raids choose one species from all five options; smaller raids are most common. Hunters also raid kaiju settlements.

Fortified walls require stage 2 for Crimson, 4 for Azure, and 5 for Void. Use Ocean breach attacks, Gila digging, Mountain lasers, Frostback stomps or Solar bolts. Gila fire cannot break fortified walls.

  1. Reach kaiju stage 6 or hunter King rank.
  2. Defeat the defenders required to destroy the heart or altar.
  3. With both hands empty, hold right-click on the rival anchor for ten seconds.

Capture keeps the buildings and terrain. New combatants belong to your faction; captured sites do not refill townsfolk. Land kaiju cannot capture submerged Ocean domains; Ocean kaiju and hunters can. Digging and other abilities are suppressed during capture.

Captured garrisons stop replacing losses during enemy presence, active fighting or a raid approach. They need 30 quiet seconds before recovery. At ordinary hunter villages, civilians have a separate refill check every 15 seconds while safe. See the exact defender rules.

Before you try again

  • No transformation: leave fortified village protection, check stage 2 and hold G for five seconds.
  • No power: check full form, energy and recharge. Mountain lasers cannot fire during ascent or knockdown.
  • No growth: check H, the required form, the target stage and the exact heart tier.
  • No Solar feeding: stop on the ground outdoors during clear daylight.
  • No new structures: explore newly generated chunks.

The port's in-game Since That Day book is updated when it is in your inventory on login. Books left in chests update after you carry them through a later login.
