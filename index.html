What is new?

Kaiju now grow through six stages. Mountain Serpent, Frostback and Solar Worm join the roster, with their own altars and eye powers. Maximum-level players can capture settlements. Each species has a unique roar.

The latest build also makes Frostback settlements less common and stops captured settlements replacing defenders during an attack.
