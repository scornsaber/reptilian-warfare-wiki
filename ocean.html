REPTILIAN WARFARE / PLAYER GUIDE

Ocean Beast

A heavy aquatic kaiju built for fighting around water.

Tide Altar

Find it among deep-ocean city ruins.

Abilities

  • Hold B to charge an explosive blue beam. Releasing too early cancels it. Power and recharge increase with growth.
  • Left-click for a claw swipe.
  • From stage 4, its walking stomp can hurt nearby enemies while moving on the ground. Standing still does not repeatedly stomp them.
  • Swim quickly, breathe underwater and step over obstacles.

Press R for its deep roar. The animation has an inhale, an open-jaw forward bellow, flared frills and a smooth recovery.

Feeding and eye

Feed near radioactive bloom. Its eye grants hunters radiation immunity, beam resistance and radiation on successful melee and ranged attacks.
