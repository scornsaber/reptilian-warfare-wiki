REPTILIAN WARFARE / PLAYER GUIDE

Mountain Serpent

A two-headed mountain kaiju with folding wings and paired purple lasers.

Mountain Altar

Find high cave mouths and ledges on mountain sides. There is no built walking route; bring climbing or flight access.

Flight and bite

Hold Jump to ascend, then release for elytra-like gliding. Hold Jump again to gain height. Both half and full forms can fly, and flight does not drain hunger. Left-click for a bite.

Twin lasers

Hold B for narrow purple beams from both mouths. They pierce creatures, cut ordinary terrain and ignite flammable blocks. They have a fixed channel limit and recharge. Fire while grounded or gliding, not during powered ascent. Fortified walls above your growth gate stop the beams.

Do not get caught

A successful kaiju hit on a flying Mountain Serpent knocks it down and disables flight and lasers until it lands, then for another three seconds. The knockdown adds no fall damage.

Feeding and eye

Feed near amethyst. The Mountain eye gives hunter players and NPCs ascent and gliding, with wings and horns. It does not grant lasers.

The in-game name is currently spelled “Mountain Serpant.”
