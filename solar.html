REPTILIAN WARFARE / PLAYER GUIDE

Solar Worm

A legless desert serpent with crown horns and golden neck fins.

Sun Altar

Look for it in desert ruins.

Movement and attacks

  • Sprint to lower the head and slither with a stronger wave down the body.
  • Full form cannot jump. Hold Jump while pushing into a wall to climb.
  • Left-click to bite and mark enemies with Sunscorched.
  • Tap B to fire one yellow lightning-like Sun Bolt. It follows your aim above and below the horizon, including while slithering.

The bolt stops on its first target or solid obstruction. Hitting a marked creature consumes the mark for a stronger bolt. Its recharge grows from about four to seven seconds between stages 2 and 6. Eligible fortified walls can be chipped at their growth gates.

Feeding and eye

Stand still on the ground in clear daylight with an open view of the sky to recover energy. Rain, overhead cover and movement prevent feeding. The Solar eye lets hunters mark enemies and consume a mark on a later successful hit for a small bonus, no more than once every two seconds. It gives crown horns and neck fins, but no Sun Bolt.
