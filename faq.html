Common questions

Quick answers for progression, powers and settlements.

Why will an altar not unlock another species?

New characters choose one kaiju species. Hunters cannot use altars. Existing saves retain previously unlocked forms.

Can eyes turn me into a beast?

Normally eyes grant hunter powers. The exception is Betrayal: with two active eyes, hold Use on a third for five seconds to become its stage-2 full kaiju.

Why am I not growing?

Check H. Stage 1 needs ten mod Townsfolk kills in half form; stage 2 needs an equal-or-larger full-kaiju kill in full form; stages 3 and 4 need hamlet and town hearts. Stage 5 needs both a city heart and a stage-5-or-6 full-kaiju kill. Heart objectives must be completed at their progression stage.

What happened to stage 11?

The maximum is now stage 6, with the old maximum power. Old saves round up to the next matching strength milestone.

Why can I not transform in a village?

Human walled villages and fortified wall stone suppress transformation. Choose your form before entering.

Why did my Mountain Serpent fall?

A successful kaiju hit can knock a flying Mountain Serpent down. Flight and lasers stay blocked until landing and for three seconds afterward; the knockdown adds no fall damage.

Why can Solar Worm not jump?

Full Solar uses hold-Jump wall climbing. Sprint lowers its head into a slither. Its half form retains ordinary movement.

Why do defenders keep coming back after a fight?

Captured settlements recover combatants during peace. In 3.7.1, enemies, active combat and raid approaches stop replacement, followed by a 30-second quiet period.

Why can Gila not capture Ocean ruins?

Capture keeps the existing terrain. Land kaiju cannot take a submerged settlement. Hunters and Ocean kaiju can.

Does armor stop radiation?

No. Hunters need an active Ocean eye. Rank and scale armor do not replace it.

Does the Gila eye stop venom?

No. It protects against fire and lava, including Gila Burn. Venom still affects gilas and Gila-eye hunters.

Why did hunters turn against me?

One active eye removes protection; two cause hostility. Inventory eyes do not count. You can remove a power with Sneak + Use on the matching eye.

Why will my crossbow not fire?

Check that you are a hunter, meet its rank requirement, wear a Scale Chestplate, and have waited for the recharge.

Where are the new or less frequent settlements?

World-generation changes apply in newly generated chunks. Already explored settlements are not moved or deleted.

Is the mod source public here?

No. This separate repository contains player guides, website files and selected artwork, not the mod source code.
