What changed

The current player guide covers Reptilian Warfare 3.7.1.

Minecraft 26.3 port

A separate 3.7.1+mc26.3 build updates the mod to Minecraft 26.3, NeoForge 26.3.0.58-beta, GeckoLib 5.5.7 and Java 25. Rendering, controls, saved data and test tools have been migrated. The field guide now explains progression, feeding, hunters, eyes and capture. The original 1.21.5 release remains separate.

3.7.1

This release packages the latest gameplay fixes and updates the version to 3.7.1. Use the same build on the server and all clients.

  • Added a distinct original roar sound for each kaiju.
  • Improved Ocean Beast's roar with a proper open jaw, a forward bellow, flared frills and a smoother return to idle.
  • Solar Worm now lowers its head and slithers when sprinting. In full form, hold Jump against a wall to climb instead of jumping.
  • Frostback now steps over obstacles like Ocean Beast.
  • Reduced Frostback settlement frequency in newly generated terrain. Existing settlements stay where they are.
  • Fixed captured settlements endlessly replacing defenders during an attack. Reinforcements pause while enemies or an active raid are nearby and resume only after 30 quiet seconds. Recent attacks remain recorded even if the last defender dies.
  • Changed Solar Worm's Sun Bolt into a yellow lightning trail and fixed aiming above and below the horizon.
  • Village hunters now respond to kaiju anywhere inside their walls and stay within the village boundary.
  • All kaiju have ways to breach fortified walls at the required growth stage: crimson at stage 2, azure at stage 4 and void at stage 5.
  • Trimmed Mountain Serpent's wing supports while keeping its folding-wing animation.
  • Fixed first-person half-form skin colors and species features. Solar Worm half-forms and Solar-eye hunters now have crown horns and golden neck fins.
  • Fixed disappearing borrowed-eye features on NPC hunters. Gila claws now match the selected tooth color.
  • Kaiju raids randomly choose a single species from the full roster, with habitat restrictions where needed.

Coming from the old 3.0.0 guide?

Read the updated six-stage progression, all five species guides, third-eye Betrayal and settlement capture. Mountain flight, Frostback’s frost and Solar’s marked-target attacks each have their own play style.
