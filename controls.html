Controls at a glance

Default bindings can be changed in Minecraft’s Controls menu.

Key or actionWhat it does
Hold GTransform into or out of full form after five seconds; requires stage 2+.
VToggle half form.
HOpen progression, hunter standing and appearance controls.
BHold for Ocean beam, Gila fire, Mountain lasers or Frostback breath. Tap for one Solar Sun Bolt.
RRoar in full form. Each species has its own sound.
Attack / left-clickOcean swipe, Gila/Mountain/Solar bite, or Frostback front-leg stomp.
SprintHunter dash (2-second recharge), full Gila dash (60 seconds), or Solar slither.
Hold Jump into a wallClimb in full Gila or Solar form.
Hold Jump, then releaseMountain half/full form or Mountain-eye hunter: ascend, then glide.
Hold Use / right-click, full GilaDig forward. Add Sneak for down or Jump for up.
Use altar, empty handUnlock its species if you are eligible and uncommitted.
Hold right-click on a defeated rival anchorMaximum level, both hands empty: capture over ten seconds. Digging and other abilities are suppressed during the attempt.
Use eye as hunterActivate a first or second distinct borrowed power.
Hold Use on a third eyeAfter five seconds, Betrayal turns you into that eye’s stage-2 full kaiju.
Sneak + Use matching eyeRemove that active eye power.
XSummon hunter troops if your effective levy rank is at least 2.

Digging or biting?

Attack to bite, Use to dig. Digging is a full-Gila ability. Holding right-click on an eligible settlement anchor starts capture instead.

If a key does nothing

Check the current form, stage, energy and recharge. Close menus and check for key conflicts. Fortified human villages can block transformation. Mountain knockdown temporarily blocks flight and lasers.
