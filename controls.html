REPTILIAN WARFARE / CONTROLS

Controls at a glance

These are the default bindings. You can change them in Minecraft’s Controls menu.

Key or actionWhat it does
Hold GChange into or out of full beast form after the five-second hold. Requires stage 2 or higher.
VToggle half form.
HOpen kaiju progression and appearance, or hunter standing.
Hold BCharge the ocean beam or channel gila fire in full form.
RRoar in beast form.
XSummon hunter troops when your effective levy rank is at least 2.
Tap Sprint (Left Ctrl by default)Dash as a hunter or full gila. It works in the air too.
Attack (left-click)Beast swipe or gila bite; ordinary weapon attacks when applicable.
Hold Use (right-click), full gilaDig forward.
Sneak + Use, full gilaDig down. Sneak is Left Shift by default.
Jump + Use, full gilaDig up. Jump is Space by default.
Jump while pushing into a wall, full gilaClimb.
Use an altar with an empty handUnlock its species if you have not chosen a side.
Use an eye as a hunterConsume it to activate that species’ borrowed power.
Sneak + use a matching eye as a hunterRemove that species’ active borrowed power.

Digging or biting?

They use different buttons: attack to bite, use to dig. Digging is a full-gila ability. NPC gilas attack with an animation but do not use player digging controls.

If a key does nothing

Close any open menu, check your form and growth stage, and wait for the ability’s recharge. In Minecraft’s Controls menu, check for conflicting bindings. Walled human villages can also block transformation.
