Borrowed eyes & effects

Choose two borrowed powers—or risk becoming the beast yourself.

EyeHunter benefit
OceanRadiation immunity, 65% Ocean beam resistance, and radiation on successful melee and ranged attacks.
GilaFire, lava, kindling and Gila Burn immunity. Successful melee and ranged attacks cause stronger Gila Burn. No venom immunity.
MountainHold Jump to ascend and release to glide. Flight costs no hunger. Gives wings and horns, not lasers.
FrostbackProtection from freezing and Chilled. Successful melee and ranged attacks chill enemies. Gives plates, not frost breath.
SolarAttacks mark enemies with Sunscorched. A following hit can consume the mark for a small bonus, at most once every two seconds. Gives crown horns and neck fins, not Sun Bolt.

First and second eyes

As a hunter, use an eye to consume it and activate its species power. The first two must be different species. Active powers—not spare inventory eyes—determine how garrisons react. Item tooltips describe each gift, and activation displays a species message.

Active eyesGarrison responseLevy strength
0Protect you as a hunter.Your rank.
1Withdraw protection without hostility just for that eye.One rank lower.
2Treat you as an enemy, including the King.Two ranks lower. Your own levy stays loyal.

Third eye: Betrayal

With two powers active, hold Use on another eye for five seconds. It can be any species, including one you already used. Completing the hold consumes it, awards Betrayal, and immediately turns you into that eye’s stage-2 full kaiju.

You lose hunter rank, borrowed-eye powers and summoned troops. You then use the new species’ abilities and normal kaiju progression. Releasing before the five seconds finishes cancels the attempt.

Remove a borrowed power

Hold the matching eye and Sneak + Use. The held eye is not consumed, but the previously consumed power is removed without refund. You can do this away from a King.

Effects

EffectWhat to expect
Abyssal radiationDamages and weakens exposed creatures. Eye attacks apply it for six seconds; radiation sources can refresh exposure.
Gila BurnA 20-second burn dealing twice normal burning damage before protections. Water or rain can extinguish it.
Gila venomBite poison that can affect other gilas and Gila-eye hunters. It has its own status icon.
ChilledStacks up to five times for up to 50% movement reduction. Stacks thaw gradually without more exposure.
SunscorchedA mark used by Solar bites and eye attacks. Solar bolts and Solar-eye follow-up hits can consume it for bonus damage.

Scale armor and hunter rank do not grant radiation immunity. Ocean beam resistance is not beam immunity. Borrowed eyes do not grant the species’ full ranged attack.
